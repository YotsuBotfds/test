Ring of Fire

Every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or larger recorded worldwide over three years. This globe has no map on it. The glowing lines are the quakes themselves, and they trace the edges of the tectonic plates. Below it, five more instruments look inside the planet.

      How this is made

      Every pixel on this page is computed by a Rust software rasteriser compiled to WebAssembly: projection, gaussian splats, anti-aliased lines, bloom and tone mapping, travel-time solvers and synthetic seismograms. There is no WebGL, no canvas drawing API beyond copying finished pixels, and no chart library. The whole catalogue is compiled into the module.

      Earthquakes: USGS ANSS ComCat, M4.5 and larger, fetched 25 Oct 2026. Coastlines: Natural Earth 1:110m. Plate boundaries: Bird (2003), PB2002.
