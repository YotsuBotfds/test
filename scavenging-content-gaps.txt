WHAT'S MISSING — ENVIRONMENT-BASED SCAVENGING GUIDES
=====================================================

The existing content is strong on TECHNICAL PROCESSING (what to do with salvage
once you have it) but weak on SOURCING (where to go, what to look for, and how
environment type changes your strategy). There is no dedicated guide that says
"you're in a rural area, here's what to prioritize" or "you're in a city,
here are your highest-value targets."


GAP 1: CITY/URBAN SCAVENGING GUIDE (does not exist)
----------------------------------------------------

Nothing currently covers systematic urban scavenging strategy. A dedicated guide
should address high-value locations and what to extract from each:

  AUTOMOTIVE (dealerships, repair shops, parking structures, junkyards)
  - Alternators, starters, batteries (lead-acid for plates + acid)
  - Belts, hoses, clamps, fasteners
  - Wiring harnesses (copper + insulation)
  - Hydraulic cylinders, brake fluid, transmission fluid
  - Tires (rubber stock), leaf springs (high-carbon steel)
  - Catalytic converters (platinum group metals)
  - Radiators (copper/aluminum), heater cores

  HOSPITALS & PHARMACIES
  - Surgical instruments (stainless steel, precision-made)
  - Antibiotics, antiseptics, anesthetics (shelf life guide needed)
  - IV tubing, syringes, suture kits
  - Oxygen tanks, regulators
  - Bedframes (steel), wheelchairs (aluminum + rubber)
  - Generator systems (backup power infrastructure)
  - Sterilization equipment (autoclaves)

  HARDWARE STORES & HOME IMPROVEMENT
  - Hand tools (hammers, saws, chisels, files, planes)
  - Fasteners bulk (nails, screws, bolts — sorted by type/size)
  - Adhesives, sealants, epoxies
  - Plumbing fittings (copper, PVC, brass valves)
  - Electrical wire spools, junction boxes, breakers
  - Rope, chain, cable
  - Propane tanks, welding supplies
  - Seeds (garden section — check viability)
  - Lumber, sheet goods, concrete mix

  OFFICE BUILDINGS & SCHOOLS
  - Paper stock (writing, printing, fire starting, insulation)
  - Magnets (hard drives, speakers)
  - Copper wiring in walls
  - Fire extinguishers (pressurized vessels + chemicals)
  - Cafeteria equipment (commercial cookware, stainless steel)
  - Lab equipment (schools — glassware, chemicals, scales)
  - Furniture (wood, metal framing)

  RESTAURANTS & COMMERCIAL KITCHENS
  - Stainless steel cookware, prep surfaces
  - Propane/natural gas lines and tanks
  - Walk-in cooler insulation panels
  - Knives, sharpening stones
  - Grease traps (rendered fat for soap)
  - Canned goods (long shelf-life stock)

  MANUFACTURING & INDUSTRIAL
  - Machine tools (lathes, drill presses, grinders)
  - Raw stock (bar, rod, sheet metal, tubing)
  - Bearings, gears, sprockets, chains
  - Electric motors (often large, high-quality copper)
  - Hydraulic/pneumatic systems
  - Welding equipment and consumables
  - Safety equipment (gloves, goggles, respirators)

  FIRE STATIONS
  - Hoses (high-pressure rated, long lengths)
  - Axes, pry bars, bolt cutters (Halligan bars)
  - Generators, pumps
  - First aid / trauma supplies
  - Turnout gear (fire-resistant clothing)
  - Portable radios, batteries

  CONSTRUCTION SITES
  - Rebar (steel reinforcement)
  - Concrete forms (reusable lumber)
  - Heavy equipment (diesel engines, hydraulics)
  - Scaffolding (pipe + clamps)
  - Portable generators, compressors

  RESIDENTIAL (apartments, houses, subdivisions)
  - Copper plumbing (older homes especially)
  - Water heaters (glass-lined steel tanks, heating elements, thermostats)
  - Appliance motors (washing machines, dryers, HVAC blowers)
  - Window glass (flat glass stock)
  - Gutters and downspouts (aluminum)
  - Garage contents (personal tool collections, fuel, lubricants)
  - Mattress springs (steel wire stock)
  - Mirrors (glass + silver backing)
  - Batteries (household, car, UPS systems)

  MISSING URBAN TOPICS (not covered anywhere):
  - Systematic neighborhood sweep methodology
  - Structural safety assessment for entering buildings
  - Load-bearing wall identification (don't collapse the building on yourself)
  - Hazardous material avoidance (asbestos, lead paint, gas leaks, sewage)
  - Marking/cataloging cleared vs uncleared buildings
  - Transport logistics (carts, sleds, staging areas)
  - Team scavenging protocols and communication
  - Time-decay priorities (what degrades first and must be grabbed early)


GAP 2: RURAL SCAVENGING GUIDE (does not exist)
-----------------------------------------------

Rural environments have completely different high-value targets. Nothing in the
current content addresses this. Key locations and targets:

  FARMS & RANCHES
  - Tractors and implements (diesel engines, hydraulics, PTO systems)
  - Fuel storage tanks (diesel, gasoline — check for water contamination)
  - Fencing (wire, posts, electric fence chargers + solar panels)
  - Barbed wire (steel wire stock, also defensive)
  - Grain bins (sheet metal, auger motors, drying fans)
  - Animal handling equipment (gates, chutes, feeders)
  - Veterinary supplies (antibiotics, suture materials, syringes)
  - Well pumps (submersible motors, pipe, pressure tanks)
  - Hay equipment (mower blades — high-carbon steel)
  - Seed stock (stored grain for planting — viability matters)
  - Irrigation systems (pipe, sprinklers, pumps, timers)
  - Workshop contents (welders, grinders, air compressors, hand tools)
  - Livestock trailers (axles, bearings, structural steel)
  - Windmills (tower steel, gearbox, pump rod)

  RURAL HARDWARE & FEED STORES
  - Bulk fasteners, fencing supplies
  - Animal feed (caloric value for humans in emergency)
  - Veterinary medicines (crossover applicability)
  - Rope, twine, baling wire
  - Chainsaw parts, bar oil, fuel mix
  - Axes, mauls, wedges, logging chains

  SAWMILLS & LUMBER YARDS
  - Seasoned lumber (already processed)
  - Sawmill blades (high-quality steel)
  - Conveyor systems (belts, rollers, motors)
  - Sawdust/wood chips (insulation, composting, fuel)
  - Drying kilns (brick/metal structures, heating elements)

  HUNTING & SPORTING GOODS
  - Firearms and ammunition
  - Optics (binoculars, scopes — precision glass)
  - Fishing gear (line, hooks, nets)
  - Camping equipment (tents, sleeping bags, cookware)
  - Knives, multitools
  - Paracord, carabiners, climbing gear

  CHURCHES & COMMUNITY BUILDINGS
  - Large cooking facilities (community kitchens)
  - Generators (backup power)
  - Bell bronze (high-value metal)
  - Large interior spaces (wood, metal roofing)
  - PA systems (speakers, amplifiers, wire)

  GRAIN ELEVATORS & SILOS
  - Bulk grain (food + seed stock)
  - Conveyor/auger systems
  - Large motors and gearboxes
  - Sheet metal (bin walls)
  - Explosion-proof electrical components

  RURAL POWER INFRASTRUCTURE
  - Utility poles (treated lumber)
  - Transformers (copper windings, transformer oil — PCB warning needed)
  - Copper and aluminum wire (transmission lines)
  - Insulators (porcelain/glass — specialized uses)
  - Solar panels on rural properties
  - Battery bank systems (off-grid homes)

  WATERWAYS & PONDS
  - Dam structures (potential micro-hydro sites)
  - Culverts (steel/concrete pipe)
  - Pump houses (motors, pipe, valves)
  - Fish stock (food source assessment)
  - Dock hardware (marine-grade fasteners, rope, chain)

  ABANDONED MINES & QUARRIES
  - Rail track (high-quality steel)
  - Mine carts (wheels, bearings, axles)
  - Ventilation equipment
  - Stone/gravel (building material)
  - Timber shoring (already cut to structural size)
  - WARNING: gas accumulation, cave-in risks, water hazards

  MISSING RURAL TOPICS (not covered anywhere):
  - How to assess a rural property quickly for value
  - Distance/transport challenges (no cart = limited haul)
  - Seasonal access issues (mud, snow, flooding)
  - Animal hazards (livestock gone feral, wildlife in structures)
  - Well and water system evaluation
  - Fuel siphoning and storage safety
  - Farm chemical hazards (fertilizer, pesticides, anhydrous ammonia)
  - Identifying viable seed stock vs degraded

GAP 4: PROGRESSION-BOOSTING FRAMEWORK (missing)
------------------------------------------------

The salvage_variants_master.json has a basic priority system, but nothing ties
scavenging directly to the skill progression tree. A guide linking "if you
find X, you can skip Y weeks of primitive manufacturing" would be huge:

  PROGRESSION SHORTCUTS VIA SALVAGE
  - Found a working axe → skip flint knapping + hafting (saves weeks)
  - Found copper wire → skip smelting, drawing wire (saves months)
  - Found a hand drill → skip bow drill mastery for fire AND tool making
  - Found antibiotics → bypass months of herbal medicine progression
  - Found a generator → shortcut entire Phase 5 electricity bootstrapping
  - Found quality steel → skip bloom iron → wrought iron → steel chain
  - Found glass jars → skip pottery progression for food storage
  - Found a sewing machine → skip hand-sewing skill development
  - Found solar panels → bypass steam/hydro power generation entirely

  This could be structured as:
  - "Salvage → Skill Skip" mapping table
  - Priority tiers based on which skips save the most time
  - Environment-specific versions (what you're likely to find rural vs city)
  - Degradation timeline (find it in month 1 vs month 12 — still viable?)


GAP 5: TIME-DECAY & PRIORITIZATION (partial — needs expansion)
--------------------------------------------------------------

salvage_variants_master.json covers first 24hr/week/month priorities but
doesn't address the degradation timeline:

  WHAT DEGRADES AND WHEN
  - Immediate (days): Fresh food, some medicines, exposed batteries
  - Weeks: Fuel (gasoline degrades, diesel lasts longer), opened chemicals
  - Months: Seeds (viability drops), rubber (UV/ozone), exposed metals (rust)
  - Years: Canned food, sealed electronics, hand tools, glass, ceramics
  - Decades: Stone, quality steel (oiled), precious metals, fired clay

  This matters because it changes WHEN you scavenge WHAT. Early runs should
  target perishable high-value items. Later runs can focus on durable stock.
